Electric Propulsion Test Engineer

El Segundo, CA

About General Galactic

General Galactic designs, builds, and flies advanced spacecraft to make life multi-planetary.

Job Description

As an Electric Propulsion Test Engineer, you will lead test campaigns for our next-generation thrusters.

Responsibilities may include:

  • Design, build, and operate vacuum test facilities.
  • Execute thruster performance and lifetime tests.
  • Analyze and report test data to engineering and leadership.

Requirements

  • B.S. in Aerospace, Mechanical, or Electrical Engineering.
  • 3+ years of hands-on propulsion test experience.

Very brief statements of fit/interest in this role are encouraged, please send to careers@gengalactic.com.
